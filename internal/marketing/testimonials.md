# Testimonial Templates

Every testimonial must be **real and verifiable** — the product's whole pitch
is honesty, so fabricated quotes would be fatal. These templates only shape
real feedback into publishable form.

## Collection prompts (ask subscribers, never fabricate)

Trigger after: 3+ weeks of Pro tenure, or a referral.

> Hi {{first_name}}, you've been receiving full setups for a few weeks now.
> In one or two sentences: what made you try Deepstream, and what has the
> walk-forward track record changed about how you evaluate signal services?
> Reply here and we may feature you (with your permission) on the site.

## Shaping real feedback into a testimonial

Raw: *"idk it's just different from those telegram pumps, they show the losses
too and the tuna trade was on point"*

Published (with permission):

> "Deepstream is different from the usual signal Telegram. They publish their
> losses alongside their wins — and the tuna setup actually came in."
> — Pro subscriber, 3 months

## Placement
- Landing page `#compare` section (short pull-quote strip).
- Email campaign #4 (Pro upgrade nudge) footer.
- Referral landing (`?ref=` pages).

## Guardrails
- Never edit meaning; only trim and fix grammar.
- Always attribute with tenure (e.g. "Pro subscriber, 3 months").
- One line: `<!-- testimonial -->` markers in `signal_site/index.html` mark
  where approved quotes slot in.
