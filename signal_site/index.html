METHODOLOGY

How a signal is produced

01

Observe

Daily NOAA sea-surface temperature indices, satellite chlorophyll concentration, and subsea chemical plume monitoring.

02

Measure

A grid-search optimizer sweeps smoothing windows and lead times to find the lag at which each ocean variable most consistently precedes price.

03

Validate

Dickey–Fuller stationarity and Granger causality tests run before any setup is published. Weak or noisy relationships are never traded.

04

Replay

The engine is replayed out-of-sample over the prior five years to build an honest track record — the same output a subscriber receives.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this financial advice?

No. Deepstream is a quantitative research service. Signals are informational and educational. You are solely responsible for your own trading decisions.

Why would the ocean predict commodities?

Physical systems precede markets. El Niño alters rainfall around South American copper mines; chlorophyll collapses signal nutrient disruption that constrains tuna catch; subsea plumes flag energy infrastructure stress. Markets price these events in after physical data — Deepstream aims to lead.

How is the track record computed?

Walk-forward replay. For each historical signal date we use only the data available up to that date, generate the exact setup a subscriber would receive, then follow the trade forward until the stop or target is hit, or 60 days pass.

What if there are no high-confidence signals?

We say so. Standing aside is a position. Deepstream never fabricates a setup to justify a subscription.

How do I receive the signals?

Via a private Telegram channel. Free subscribers get the weekly summary on the public channel.
